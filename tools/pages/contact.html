Contact

4square is made by Kyle Mathewson

Questions, bug reports, level ideas and feedback are all welcome.

Bugs and suggestions

Open an issue on the game's public repository: github.com/kylemath/4Square/issues. Please say which level and which game style you were playing, and what device and browser you use.

Your data

To delete your leaderboard entries, use the account screen (🏆) in the game. For anything else about your data, including deletion of research records, open an issue as above. See the privacy page for what the game stores.

Research and press

If you study puzzles, graph colouring or how people learn, and would like to work with the game's levels or its anonymous play data, get in touch the same way. The research behind the levels is at GraphColourDeductiveSolver.
